1. "Month-end takes two weeks because stock and books never match."
2. "Warehouse staff overwrite each other's spreadsheets."
3. "We can't tell which products are actually profitable."
4. "Payroll errors every cut-off; shifts tracked on paper."
5. "Auditor asks who changed an entry — we can't answer."
6. "Owner wants to see cash and sales on his phone."
